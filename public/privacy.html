Privacy Policy

The short version: GeoQuest collects as little as it can, never sells anything, shows no ads, and lets you delete your account and data yourself.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

GeoQuest (playgeoquest.app) is run by Aadi (“we”). This page explains what information the app handles when you use it, why, who helps us run it, how long it’s kept and your choices. Questions? Use Send feedback in the app.

Like every website, our hosting provider also briefly keeps standard request logs (which include internet addresses) for security and reliability. We use your internet address to apply rate limits that protect the service from abuse; we don’t store it with your account or visits.

We don’t sell or rent your information, we don’t use it for advertising, and we don’t share it with anyone except the providers below, who help run GeoQuest — or if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage on your device

GeoQuest uses only cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, and — only if you add them yourself — cookies holding your own Anthropic or Mapillary key, sealed so only the server can read them. There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies, so there’s no cookie banner.

The app also keeps a few things in your browser’s storage — your settings, a guest’s Daily result, recently used quiz countries — and caches the app so it opens quickly and works offline. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Who helps run GeoQuest

  • Vercel (United States) — hosting, storage, security and request logs.
  • Anthropic — the Claude AI behind Ask the Atlas and answer checking. Your questions (and the country you’re looking at) are sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. The Atlas may search the web to answer, so the topic of your question may be sent on to a search provider.
  • Ably — real-time connections for live Duels.
  • Mapillary (Meta) and Panoramax — street-level imagery in the Street View game, loaded directly by your browser.
  • OpenFreeMap, Wikipedia and flagcdn — map tiles, place summaries and photos, and flag images, loaded directly by your browser.
  • Google Fonts — the app’s typefaces, loaded directly by your browser.
  • Your browser’s push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) — only if you turn on reminders.

When your browser loads something from these services, they receive your internet address and basic browser details, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: signed in, open the menu, tap your name, then Delete my account. This removes your account, places, favourites, Daily results and streak, reminders and visit history straight away. Your name may remain in other players’ Duel history, and feedback you sent stays with us unless you ask us to remove it.
  • See, correct or remove your information, or ask a question about it: use Send feedback and leave an email so we can reply. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, LGPD, KVKK, India’s DPDP Act or US state laws) you may have further rights, such as access, correction, portability or objection — we’ll honour them.
  • Use GeoQuest without an account: you can explore and play the Daily as a guest.
  • Turn off reminders at any time from 🔔 Reminder in the menu, or in your browser or phone settings.

Security

Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), stored data is private to the service, and access to it is limited to running GeoQuest. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe and will act promptly if anything goes wrong.

Where your information is kept

GeoQuest runs on Vercel’s servers in the United States, so your information is stored and processed there, wherever you use the app from.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top; for significant changes we’ll make it clear in the app.
