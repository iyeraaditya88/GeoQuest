About GeoQuest

A living 3D globe for getting to know the world — and a handful of games that make it stick.

What it is

GeoQuest started from a simple idea: geography is more fun on a globe you can spin than on a list you memorise. Click any country for its capital, people, languages and a few things worth remembering. Then put yourself to the test:

  • Daily challenge — the same five countries for everyone, every day. Find them on the globe and share your squares.
  • Map quiz — find countries, name flags, or work from GeoGuessr-style clues.
  • Street View — dropped somewhere on Earth: where are you?
  • Capitals, Geo Trivia and Name the Top 5 — quick rounds that get harder as you get better.
  • Duel — play friends live, head-to-head, with a scoreboard and chat.
  • Ask the Atlas — ask anything, and the answer lights up on the globe.

You can also browse every country — its map, capital, neighbours and GeoGuessr clues. It’s free, it works in any modern browser, and you can install it on your phone like an app. No account is needed to explore or play the Daily; a free account adds duels, streaks and saved places.

Who makes it

GeoQuest is made by Aadi — an independent project, built for people who love maps, travel and trivia. Every suggestion and bug report is read, and many have already shaped the app.
