# Search (SEO)

Pages are generated at build time from the app's own data (see `scripts/country-pages.ts`).

| Pages | Targets | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `/country/<name>` (245) | "<country> capital", "<country> facts", "where is <country>" | Live |
| `/wordle/<n>` (past days only) | "geo wordle answer", "geo wordle yesterday" | Planned |
| `/quiz/<continent>`, `/capitals/<continent>` | "countries of Africa quiz", "Europe capitals quiz" | Planned |

**Your one-time step:** add playgeoquest.app to Google Search Console and submit
`https://playgeoquest.app/sitemap.xml`.
