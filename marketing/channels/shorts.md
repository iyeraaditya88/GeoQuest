# Short video: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

- **Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 15–25 s, no music needed (add a trending sound in the app if you like).
- **First 2 seconds** decide everything: the burned-in hook line is a question or a surprising number.
- **Caption:** 1–2 short lines + a call to try it + 3–5 hashtags. Link in bio on TikTok/Instagram
  (put the tagged link there); YouTube Shorts can carry the link in the description.
- **Hashtags to rotate:** #geography #maps #geoguessr #worldle #dailypuzzle #trivia #learnontiktok
  #travel #history #didyouknow
- **Post time:** whenever suits you — consistency beats timing. Same caption works on all three;
  the pack still gives one per channel so each carries its own tracked link.

caption limits: tiktok 300, reels 300, shorts 100
