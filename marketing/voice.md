# Voice

**Who we're talking to:** curious people who like geography, puzzles and travel — commuters doing a
daily puzzle, students and teachers, GeoGuessr and Wordle players. Mostly on phones.

**What GeoQuest is, in one line:** a free 3D globe you can play — daily puzzles, a detective chase
across the world, and a map you can actually explore. No sign-up needed.

**Tone:** warm, curious, a little playful. Like a friend who just learned a wild fact and has to
tell you. Short sentences. One idea per post. Show, don't hype.

**Do**
- Lead with something surprising and true ("Chile is 4,300 km long but only 177 km wide").
- Ask the viewer to try: "Can you find it in 3 tries?", "How fast can you crack it?"
- Use real numbers and names from the app's own data.
- End with the link (it's added automatically, tagged per channel).

**Don't**
- Claim things the app doesn't do, or invent facts.
- Use hype words: "revolutionary", "ultimate", "best ever", "insane", "game-changer".
- Mention other brands' games or characters.

## Hard rules (the validator refuses captions that break these)
- Never mention "Carmen Sandiego" (or Carmen, V.I.L.E., ACME).
- Never reveal **today's** Daily, Geo Wordle or Mystery answers. Yesterday's and older are fine.
- No fake reviews, testimonials or invented player numbers.
- On Reddit and in communities, always say you're the person who made it.
- Every link carries its `?ref=` tag (the scripts add it — don't strip it).

banned: carmen sandiego, carmen, v.i.l.e., acme, revolutionary, ultimate, best ever, insane, game-changer
