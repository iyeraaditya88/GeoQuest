table by weekday. Scenes: `mystery`, `wordle-yesterday`, `fact`, `empires`, `voyages`, `eras`.

| Day | Scene | Angle |
|---|---|---|

| Tuesday | fact | One surprising country fact, flown to on the globe |
| Wednesday | wordle-yesterday | Yesterday's Geo Wordle answer revealed — "did you get it?" |
| Thursday | empires | How big an empire really was, on the globe |
| Friday | mystery | A different case, a different chase |
| Saturday | voyages | A famous voyage, sailed in 20 seconds |
| Sunday | eras | Where humans lived, era by era |
